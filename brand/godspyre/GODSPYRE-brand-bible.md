# GODSPYRE
### *Gods of War. Gods of Survival.*

Premium streetwear house fusing **mid-1800s oil-painting war** (Napoleonic/Crimean smoke,
brass, regimental banners, blood and mud) with **Game-of-Thrones mythic heraldry** — worn
with the swagger of a Gen-Z deity who survived the battle and wears the ash as a flex.

**Name logic:** *God* (the immortal) + *Pyre* (the funeral fire of fallen warriors).
Gods who burn and don't die. Survival as religion.

---

## 1. Brand Archetype
- **Primary — The Warrior-Hero:** courage, mastery, earned glory.
- **Overlay — The Ruler:** legacy, heraldry, premium authority (justifies the price).
- **Shadow — The Magician:** death-and-rebirth, myth-making.

## 2. Brand Personality
| Trait | On the body |
|---|---|
| Mythic | Each drop feels like a relic dug from a battlefield. |
| Battle-worn, not broken | Distress is dignity; scars are status. |
| Reverent & defiant | Worships the fallen; bows to no one living. |
| Premium-heavy | Garment weight, foil, embroidery — it *feels* like armor. |
| Quietly violent | Restraint, then one devastating detail. |

## 3. Tone of Voice — *Scripture meets street*
Short. Commanding. Carved-in-stone cadence. Never tries too hard.
- **Do:** "Born of ash. Worn by gods." · "Survive the drop." · "No retreat. No refund on glory."
- **Don't:** exclamation spam, hypebeast clichés, over-description.
- **Rule:** if a line wouldn't survive being chiseled into brass, cut it.

## 5. Primary Fonts
- **Display / wordmark:** imperial engraved serif (Trajan/Ogg lineage; accessible: **Cinzel**). ALL CAPS, wide tracking.
- **Accent:** refined blackletter (e.g. Cloister Black) — one word max, ever.
- **Functional / body:** neutral grotesk (**Söhne** / **Neue Haas**; free: **Archivo** / **Inter**).
- **Rule:** serif for myth, grotesk for function. Never two display faces in one lockup.

## 6. Logo Structure
1. **The Seal** (logomark/icon) — circular stamped war-god medallion. Hero asset.
2. **Primary lockup** — Seal above the GODSPYRE wordmark.
3. **Horizontal lockup** — Seal left, wordmark right (web header, woven label).
4. **Monogram** — single **G** in the seal ring, or **GS** — hardware, app icon, embroidery.

## 7. Logomark / Icon
Coin-die war-god death-mask (great-helm, hollow eyes) crowned by a pyre flame, flanked by two
furled regimental banners, crossed sabres beneath, ringed by an engraved seal with GODSPYRE
arched along the bottom. Designed to survive shrinking to a 6mm metal rivet.

## 8. Logomark Integration
- Tonal Ash-on-Ash seal, left chest (quiet luxury) **or** brass-thread on back yoke (statement).
- Oversized oxblood+brass foil/puff print, full chest.
- Debossed seal on leather hem patch; engraved zipper pulls/buttons.
- Monogram as app icon/favicon; seal as loader endcap.

## 9. Logo Usage Rules
- **Clear space:** ≥ the height of the flame on all sides.
- **Min size:** Seal ≥ 14mm print / 32px screen; below that use the monogram.
- **Backgrounds:** Ash Black, Bone, or Oxblood only. Never on busy photography without a scrim.
- **Approved combos:** Brass-on-Ash · Bone-on-Ash · Ash-on-Bone · Brass-on-Oxblood.
- **Never:** recolor outside palette · stretch/skew · drop shadow/gradient/glow · rotate · pure white · hand-rebuild the seal.

## 10. Graphics Motifs
Regimental banners · cannon-smoke & ash · heraldic sigils & wax seals · crossed sabres/bayonets ·
laurel wreaths · ravens · **gold-leaf kintsugi cracks** on armor (survival made beautiful) ·
engraving/intaglio shading · halftone oil-painting texture · burnt edges & foil distress.

## 11. Layout Planning
- 12-col grid, generous margins. Centered heraldic symmetry for hero moments; hard-left brutalist
  grotesk for utility (sizes/info).
- Hierarchy: Seal → scripture line (serif) → grotesk support → oxblood micro-accent.
- Negative space = battlefield smoke. Drama lives in the emptiness around one emblem.
- Photography: desaturated, warm-shadow, single brass key-light, film grain.

## 12. Real-Life Presentation
Heavyweight 280–320gsm boxy tees, garment-dyed Ash Black & Bone; oxblood capsule. Woven seal
labels, debossed leather hem patch, wax-seal hangtags on waxed cord, ash-grey tissue + matte-black
box with foil seal. Lookbook shot like a war oil-painting.
